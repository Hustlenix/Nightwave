## Required before design/funding submission

The hardware-specific page distinguishes digital design funding from later build
submission. It requires an understandable README, assembly/PCB images, a finished
replicable design, complete electronics-inclusive CAD with real attachment
methods, firmware source even if untested, linked BOM CSV with total, PCB sources,
STEP and editable CAD sources, and organized relevant files. Another person must
sanity-check the design and their findings must be addressed.

Authorship is mandatory: the design must be original and by the builder, not AI.
The page warns fully AI-generated design files may be permanently rejected.
AI disclosure and clicking approval on AI output cannot be assumed sufficient.
Builder-created electrical/mechanical work and its provenance are still needed;
obtain program guidance if the proposed assistance boundary is unclear.

## Required later for final build submission

The hardware page additionally requires genuine build-progress journals/photos,
finished-build photos and demo video in README, and source changes matching any
assembly bodges. The general submission pages require tracked human work,
journals, demo and AI notes. Do not substitute a design render for a build photo.
